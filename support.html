如何开始练习？

进入“情境”，选择一关。每段对话有四轮，从开场到收尾，台词出现在说话人物头顶。

  1. 先读或听对方的话，按需要查看中文。
  2. 选择一个回应。选错会显示原因，留在本轮直接重选。
  3. 选对后，对方接话并自动进入下一轮。
  4. 完成第四轮后，回看完整交流与表达总结。

也可以使用角色排练、表达回忆卡、参考资料和关联行为面试题。

怎样设置英语声音？听不到朗读怎么办？

对话支持自动朗读，可在 App“语音设置”中关闭，并随时手动播放。在同一设置中选择本机已有的英语声音；需要添加声音时，可在 iPhone 的辅助功能设置中下载增强或优质英语声音。

朗读由 Apple 系统合成语音生成，并非演员录音。可以使用慢速朗读帮助跟读。如果听不到声音，请检查 iPhone 音量、蓝牙或耳机的输出位置，以及所选英语声音是否可用。

无法录音，或录音突然停止？

在 iPhone“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 麦克风”中检查权限。录音只在主动点击后开始，每段最多 60 秒，用于本机回听，没有语音识别或自动评分。

开始新录音会替换上一段。离开练习、进入后台、系统音频中断或耳机断开时，声音操作会停止并清理临时录音。请在回听完成前留在练习页。

换手机如何继续？导入会覆盖记录吗？

在“成长”中选择“导出学习备份”，把 JSON 文件保存在你选择的位置。在另一台设备安装 App 后，选择“导入学习备份”。

确认导入会替换当前设备的收藏、进度和故事。建议先导出当前记录。临时录音不包含在备份中。

如果导入失败，请保留原文件并使用未编辑的 App 导出备份重试。不要把包含私人故事的完整备份发给客服。

积分和复习时间代表什么？

积分记录练习参与，不是英语水平评分或考试成绩。复习间隔根据你对主动回忆的自评安排；提前反复答对不会不断推迟下次复习。

怎样删除记录或保护自己的故事？

可以在故事编辑器中清空内容，取消表达收藏。卸载 App 会移除设备上的 App 数据；iPhone 系统备份和自行导出的文件需在相应位置管理。

尽量用化名或概括性表述描述真实项目，避免写入客户、同事或研究参与者的识别信息。开发者不能远程读取或删除你设备上的故事。

How do I start practising?

Open the Scenarios tab (情境) and choose a lesson. Each conversation has four rounds, with dialogue above the speaking character.

  1. Read or listen to the other person, and view the Chinese translation when useful.
  2. Choose a reply. An incorrect choice explains why and keeps you in the same round to try again.
  3. A correct choice shows the other person’s response and moves on automatically.
  4. After round four, review the full exchange and its takeaways.

You can also use role rehearsal, expression cards, reference material and related behavioural interview questions.

Can I download it now? Is there a charge?

The current version is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. The app has not launched publicly. If you have received a TestFlight invitation, use the link in that invitation.

The initial public release is planned for New Zealand, Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada. Public availability will be confirmed at launch.

How do I choose a voice or change automatic speech?

In Voice Settings, you can turn automatic dialogue speech off and choose from the English voices already available on your iPhone. You can still play lines manually. Additional enhanced or premium English voices can be downloaded in the accessibility settings on your iPhone.

Playback uses Apple’s system speech synthesis, not recordings by actors. A slower speech option is available for practice. If you cannot hear it, check your iPhone volume, Bluetooth or headphone output, and whether the selected English voice is available.

Why won’t recording start, or why did it stop?

Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone on your iPhone. Recording starts only when you request it, lasts up to 60 seconds and is for local playback. There is no speech recognition or automatic scoring.

A new recording replaces the previous one. Leaving practice, entering the background, an audio interruption or disconnected headphones stops audio and clears the temporary recording. Stay on the practice page until you have finished listening back.

How do I move to another phone? Does importing replace my records?

In Growth (成长), choose Export learning backup (导出学习备份) and save the JSON file somewhere you choose. On the other device, use Import learning backup (导入学习备份).

Confirming an import replaces the current device’s saved expressions, progress and stories. Export the current records first if you need to keep them. Temporary recordings are not included.

If importing fails, keep the original file and retry with an unedited app export. Do not email a complete backup containing private stories to support.

What do points and review dates mean?

Points record participation, not your English proficiency or an exam score. Review intervals follow your own assessment of recall. Repeating an early review successfully does not continually push the next review further away.

How can I remove records or protect my stories?

Clear text in the story editor or remove saved expressions. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data; manage system backups and exported files separately where they are stored.

Use made-up names or general descriptions for real projects. Avoid identifying clients, colleagues or research participants. The developer cannot remotely read or delete stories on your device.
