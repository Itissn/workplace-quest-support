PRIVACY / 隐私政策

隐私，从清楚说明开始。

App 内的学习记录由你保管。主动发送的客服邮件、Apple 测试服务和网站托管信息则按各自用途处理。

1. 谁提供服务，政策适用于什么？

AUNIQ 由 TIMEAWAKE LIMITED 提供。本政策说明 iPhone App、本支持网站及你主动联系我们时的信息处理方式。生效日期为 2026-09-15。客服及隐私联系邮箱为 info@timeawake.co.nz。

2. App 中的学习资料与记录

学习资料随 App 提供，无需注册账号。练习进度、收藏、复习安排、偏好和你编辑的面试故事保存在设备上的 App 数据中。当前 App 没有向开发者服务器上传这些记录的接口，也没有广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

开发者不会通过 App 自动读取你的故事或学习备份。你主动导出、使用系统备份，或联系支持时选择提供的信息，按下文分别处理。

3. 麦克风、临时录音与系统朗读

只有你主动选择录音时，App 才会请求麦克风权限。每段录音最多 60 秒，仅用于本机回听，不进行语音识别或自动评分，也不上传给开发者。

新录音会替换上一段。你可以主动删除；离开练习、进入后台或发生相关音频中断时，App 会停止声音操作并清理临时录音。下次启动也会清理可能残留的临时文件。录音不包含在导出的学习备份中。可在 iPhone 设置中调整麦克风权限。

英文朗读使用 Apple 系统合成语音。可用声音及离线可用性取决于设备设置和已经下载的语音；下载语音由系统处理。

4. 主动导出、系统备份与外部链接

只有你主动导出或导入学习备份时，App 才打开相应的系统文件界面。文件保存在哪里、交给谁，由你选择。导入经你确认后，会替换当前设备的学习记录。请自行管理导出文件的访问权限和删除。

iPhone 的系统备份或你选择的云盘、邮件和接收应用，按各自的设置与隐私规则处理文件。我们不控制这些服务，也不能远程读取或删除设备数据。

帮助和隐私政策页面是可选外部链接，只有点击后才打开相关网站。App 不自动加载外部网页或媒体；相关网站适用其自身隐私政策。

5. 你主动发送的客服邮件

如果你写信到 info@timeawake.co.nz，我们会收到你的发件邮箱、邮件中显示的姓名、正文以及你主动附上的文件。你也可以选择提供场景编号、复现步骤、设备型号和系统版本。

我们使用这些信息回复请求、排查问题、进行必要跟进，并归纳反馈以改进产品。客服邮件不会让你自动加入营销订阅；我们不出售这些信息，也不把它们用于广告定向。

请不要发送私人面试故事、录音、完整学习备份、密码、证件或他人的个人资料。截图请先遮盖私人内容。我们不会因为普通使用问题要求你提供这些敏感内容。

6. Apple 与 TestFlight 提供的信息

如果使用 TestFlight 测试版，Apple 会按测试服务规则处理测试信息、使用与崩溃数据，并可能向开发者提供测试诊断和你提交的反馈。TestFlight 崩溃报告可自动与开发者共享；不能将测试版理解为完全不向开发者提供任何数据。有关范围及控制方式见 Apple 的 TestFlight 与隐私说明。

如果 Apple 根据你的系统选择向我们提供 App Store 版本的诊断，我们仅将其用于排查故障与改进稳定性。App 本身仍未集成额外的分析或崩溃收集 SDK。

7. 支持网站与托管日志

本网站为静态页面，没有账号、在线反馈表单或营销订阅，不加载分析脚本、广告、外部字体或嵌入式视频。页面本身不设置分析或广告 Cookie。通过 GitHub Pages 公开托管时，GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者 IP 地址；托管服务也可能处理提供页面所需的请求信息。详见 GitHub 隐私声明。

8. 服务提供商、保存与删除

邮件收发及网站托管需要相应服务提供商处理信息，处理地点可能因服务与设置而位于不同国家或地区。我们仅为服务交付、支持、安全或适用义务处理相关信息，不把客服资料出售或分享给广告商。

设备学习记录由你管理：可清空故事、取消收藏或卸载 App。系统备份及自行导出的文件需要另行管理。临时录音按第 3 节说明清理。

客服邮件与开发者收到的诊断仅在处理请求、合理跟进、排查问题或履行适用义务所需的期间保留；不再需要时删除或去除身份关联。邮件服务或系统备份中的副本受相应服务的保存规则影响。

9. 你的选择与隐私请求

你可以通过 info@timeawake.co.nz 请求访问、更正或删除我们持有的、与你有关的客服信息，也可以提出隐私问题。为保护信息，我们可能需要合理核实请求来自本人，例如请你从原发件邮箱回复；请不要主动发送身份证件。若某些信息因适用义务仍需保留，我们会说明。

我们不能从你的手机远程读取、恢复或删除本地学习记录。设备及导出文件的管理方式见第 4、8 节。若对我们的处理有疑问，请先联系我们；也可向所在地的隐私监管机构了解申诉渠道，新西兰可咨询 Office of the Privacy Commissioner。

10. 政策更新

本政策对应当前实现与支持方式。若未来数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新此页的生效日期与相应说明，并同步检查 App Store 隐私信息。任何实际变化将另行清楚说明。

PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy starts with a clear explanation.

Your app records are yours to manage. Support emails, Apple testing services and website-hosting information are handled for their separate purposes.

The app does not upload learning records through its own interfaces. That does not mean the support mailbox, TestFlight or website hosting processes no information.

1. Who provides the service and what does this cover?

AUNIQ is provided by TIMEAWAKE LIMITED. This policy covers the iPhone app, this support website and information you choose to send us. It takes effect on 15 September 2026. For support or privacy questions, contact info@timeawake.co.nz.

2. Learning content and records in the app

Learning content is included with the app and no account is required. Progress, saved expressions, review schedules, preferences and interview stories you write are stored in the app’s data on your device. The current app has no interface for uploading those records to a developer server and includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

We do not automatically read your stories or learning backups through the app. Exports, system backups and information you choose to send for support are handled as described below.

3. Microphone, temporary recordings and system speech

The app requests microphone permission only when you choose to record. A recording lasts up to 60 seconds and is used for local playback. There is no speech recognition or automatic scoring, and recordings are not uploaded to us.

A new recording replaces the previous one. You can delete it yourself. Leaving practice, entering the background or a relevant audio interruption stops audio and clears the temporary recording. The next app launch also clears a file that may remain after an interrupted process. Recordings are excluded from learning-backup exports. You can change microphone permission in iPhone Settings.

English playback uses Apple’s system speech synthesis. Available voices and offline availability depend on device settings and installed voices. Voice downloads are managed by the operating system.

4. Exports, system backups and external links

The app opens the relevant system file interface only when you choose to export or import a learning backup. You choose where a file is saved or whom to share it with. Confirmed imports replace learning records on the current device. Manage exported files, access permissions and deletion in their chosen location.

iPhone system backups and any cloud storage, email or receiving app you select handle files under their own settings and privacy rules. We do not control those services or remotely read or delete your device data.

Links to help and the privacy policy open only when you select them. The app does not automatically load external webpages or media. Those websites apply their own privacy policies.

5. Emails you choose to send us

If you email info@timeawake.co.nz, we receive your sending address, any name shown in the email, the message and files you deliberately attach. You may also provide a scenario number, reproduction steps, device model and operating-system version.

We use that information to respond, investigate an issue, follow up where needed and identify improvements from feedback. Contacting support does not enrol you in marketing. We do not sell this information or use it for targeted advertising.

Please do not send private interview stories, recordings, complete learning backups, passwords, identity documents or other people’s personal information. Cover private details in screenshots. We do not require this sensitive content to handle ordinary usage questions.

6. Information provided through Apple and TestFlight

If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple handles testing information, usage and crash data under its testing-service rules and may provide us with diagnostics and feedback you submit. TestFlight crash reports can be shared with the developer automatically; a beta should not be understood as providing us with no information at all. See Apple’s TestFlight & Privacy notice for the scope and available controls.

If Apple provides diagnostics for an App Store version under your system choices, we use them to investigate faults and improve stability. The app itself still includes no additional analytics or crash-collection SDK.

7. The support website and hosting logs

These are static pages with no account, online feedback form or marketing subscription. They load no analytics scripts, advertisements, external fonts or embedded video, and the pages themselves set no analytics or advertising cookies. When served through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security and may process request information needed to deliver the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Service providers, retention and deletion

Email delivery and website hosting require service providers to process information. Processing locations may vary across countries or regions with the service and its settings. We handle relevant information for service delivery, support, security or applicable obligations, and do not sell support information or share it with advertisers.

You manage learning records on your device by clearing stories, removing saved expressions or uninstalling the app. Manage system backups and exported files separately. Temporary recordings are cleared as described in section 3.

Support emails and diagnostics we receive are retained only as needed to handle requests, reasonably follow up, investigate issues or meet applicable obligations, then deleted or de-identified when no longer needed. Copies in email services or system backups are subject to those services’ retention arrangements.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

You can contact info@timeawake.co.nz to request access to, correction of or deletion of support information we hold about you, or to ask a privacy question. To protect that information, we may reasonably verify that a request comes from you, for example by asking you to reply from the original email address. Please do not send identity documents without being asked. We will explain if applicable obligations require information to be retained.

We cannot remotely read, restore or delete learning records on your phone. See sections 4 and 8 for device records and exported files. Contact us with concerns about our handling; you may also ask your local privacy regulator about complaint options. In New Zealand, see the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current implementation and support arrangements. If data handling changes, we will update this page, its effective date and the corresponding App Store privacy information. Any actual change will be explained clearly.
