WORKPLACE ENGLISH, ONE CONVERSATION AT A TIME

Turn what you want to say
into words you can use.

Practise interviews, meetings and everyday collaboration. Work through a conversation from its opening to its closing, and learn what makes a response clear and useful.

How to practise ↗How your information is handled
  • iPhone · iOS 17+
  • Current version: free
  • No account required
Preparing for public release

The app is not yet publicly available on the App Store. The initial release is planned for New Zealand, Australia, the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada. Availability will be confirmed when it launches.

01

Speak in a situation

Explore 66 original scenarios, each with four rounds. Character dialogue, expressions and replies help you see the consequences of different responses.

02

Listen, then try it yourself

Use system English speech, role rehearsal and up to 60 seconds of temporary on-device recording to listen back to your own words.

03

Explain your experience

Learn with 1,188 bilingual expressions and review cards. Turn real research, project and collaboration experience into stories for 132 behavioural interview questions.

Your practice, on your device

Your learning records stay in the app on your device unless you choose to export them or use a system backup service. Choose your own pace, save useful expressions and back up your work when you need to.
